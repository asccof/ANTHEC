## Proven finding and bounded correction

An independent annual assessment can retain draft dates until final approval. POL-02 requires annual validity to begin at final administrative approval/issuance; otherwise a delayed decision can lose validity or even issue an already-expired right. On the accepted baseline, both new annual-date regressions failed; the three other new joined tests passed. The bounded correction derives annual individual/institution dates at final approval, copies effective dates to the case and preserves proposed/effective dates in its audit event. Courses retain their approved duration. Existing rights/PDFs are not migrated. AR/FR/EN screens explain the rule. Closure still requires final-head tests/CI/review; see ADR-020.
