# Approved ANTHEEC policies and implementation authority

Version: owner-2026-10-10-v1. Authority: owner's current implementation instruction and supplied consolidated ANTHEEC.docx v1.0 (SRC-08; hash in requirements/sources.json). Source attachment remains outside this public repository. This register is a faithful engineering summary, not a legal compliance certificate.

Current owner decisions take precedence over Executive Specification v1, DDD-001 and the previously proposed contracts. Earlier commits preserve their historical wording; they are not current business authority where they conflict with this register. ANTHEEC remains independent of MHIOS.

| Policy | Approved rule | Supersedes / resolves |

| POL-01 | General member 100 MAD/year; affiliated 500 (reference 1000), advanced 1500 (3000), accredited 3000 (6000). Lower tiers have no automatic accreditation. Founder/honorary is free, without annual expiry, requires administrative decision, may be suspended/cancelled; founder status is independent of offices. Versioned effective prices; fixed payable quote retained; no retrospective change to accepted payments or existing terms. Founder/honorary remain free. Strong passwords/sessions, audit, immediate removal of access, safe recovery, protected backups and restricted internal files remain required. ## Supplemental readiness decision

Design, development and tests with synthetic data only are authorized. Real-person testing/operations and production deployment are prohibited until independently verified legal/security readiness. Technical completion alone is not launch approval. Paid services and external delivery remain outside the current authorization.

## Remaining matters

Legal/security gates: detailed retention schedule; applicable CNDP procedures; privacy/processing notices; hosting and cross-border transfer review; minor safeguards; deletion/archive/restore and security readiness. Operational settings: private independent accreditation assessment/fees, event conditions and individual exceptions. Technical choices: unified exclusive calendar boundary, verification entropy/rate limits, account-linked deduplication, protected primary-account recovery, transactional issuance/outbox. The source explicitly allows technical choices during design/testing; these are not new organizational approvals.
